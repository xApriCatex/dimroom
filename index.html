Dimroom

A calm migraine and headache diary for iPhone. Log a headache in two taps, see your month at a glance, and take a clear report to your doctor.

Help and contact

Questions, problems or ideas: dimroom.app@outlook.com. Please say which iPhone and which version of Dimroom you have (Settings in the app).

Common questions

"How's today?" or "Log a headache"? "How's today?" rates the day as a whole, for a constant or background headache. "Log a headache" records one attack with a start and an end. You can use both.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Make a backup file in Settings and keep it somewhere safe, such as Files or email.

Moving to a new phone: make a backup file in Settings on the old phone, then restore it in Settings on the new one.

Dimroom Plus: manage or cancel it in the iPhone Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions. To get it back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" on the Plus screen.
