Privacy policy

For the Dimroom iPhone app. Last updated 24 October 2026.

The short version

Everything you enter in Dimroom stays on your iPhone. There is no account, and we don't collect, see, sell or share your data.

Who we are

Dimroom is made by Milad Sharhani, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: dimroom.app@outlook.com.

What stays on your phone

Your headaches, daily ratings, medicines, symptoms, triggers and notes are stored only in the app on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

When data leaves your phone

  • Only when you choose to send it: a doctor report (PDF), a spreadsheet export or a backup file goes wherever you send it with the share sheet, such as email, Files or AirDrop.
  • Dimroom Plus is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns from Apple whether Plus is active. We never see your name or payment details.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete everything" in Settings, or delete the app. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Dimroom is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Not medical advice

Dimroom is a diary. It does not diagnose, predict or give treatment advice. Talk to your doctor about your headaches and medicines.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Your rights

Questions about privacy: dimroom.app@outlook.com. In the UK you can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
